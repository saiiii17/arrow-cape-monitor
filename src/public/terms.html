What the service does

It sends a message you have written to chats you have chosen, from your own WhatsApp account, one chat at a time. It does not provide the WhatsApp account, the contacts, or the right to message them.

Your side of it

  • You have the right to message the people you add to a list, and they expect to hear from you
  • You will not use it for unsolicited marketing, anything unlawful, or anything designed to mislead
  • You keep your password safe and tell us if you think someone else has it
  • One account is one person. Do not share sign-in details
  • You remain bound by WhatsApp's own terms

Our side of it

  • We will run the service with reasonable care and keep your data as described in our privacy page
  • We will keep each customer's data separate from every other customer's
  • We will tell you before changing what you pay
  • We will give you your data if you ask, and delete it if you ask

What we cannot promise

WhatsApp can restrict your account. This service drives WhatsApp Web rather than an official business API. Bulk or automated messaging is against the spirit of WhatsApp's terms, and they may limit or ban a number that does it. We pace sends and cap broadcast sizes to reduce that risk, but we cannot remove it and we are not liable if it happens.

  • We cannot promise every message arrives. WhatsApp decides that, not us
  • We cannot promise the service is always available, though we will tell you when it is not
  • Recall works only within the roughly two days WhatsApp allows, and not for anyone who already read it

Suspension

We may suspend an account that is being used for unsolicited or unlawful messaging, or in a way that puts other customers at risk. We will say why.

Ending it

You may stop at any time: unlink your WhatsApp, or ask us to delete your account. We will give reasonable notice if we need to stop providing the service, and your data will be available to export before it is deleted.

Liability

We are responsible for failing to take reasonable care of your data. We are not responsible for what you send, who you send it to, or action WhatsApp takes against your account.
