Privacy and data protection

What we hold, why, who else can see it, and how to have it deleted.

The short version. We hold your WhatsApp messages and contacts so the service can work. We do not sell them, advertise against them, or use them to train anything. Each customer's data is kept apart from every other customer's. Ask us to delete your account and it goes, along with your lists and your message history.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your data, or share it for anyone else's marketing
  • Advertise against it, or place advertising or tracking cookies
  • Use your messages or contacts to train machine-learning models
  • Read your chats except where needed to run or fix the service
  • Message anyone on your behalf unless you pressed Send, or posted in a broadcast group you set up

Keeping customers apart

Each account's WhatsApp runs in its own process, with its own storage, named for that account. One customer's session holds no path to another's data, and the administrator's own monitoring data sits outside every customer's reach. Access is decided before any request is handled, and it denies by default.

That separation is enforced by the software and covered by automated tests that try to cross it, not by policy alone.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, delete it, or send it to you in a portable form. Write to the contact address above and we will answer within 30 days.

You can delete most of it yourself: unlinking your WhatsApp removes the session, removing a chat from a list removes it, and asking us to delete your account removes everything.

Security

  • Everything travels over HTTPS
  • Passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes
  • Administrator accounts require a second factor from an authenticator app
  • Sign-in attempts are rate limited
  • Suspending an account signs it out immediately, not when its session expires

No service can promise it will never be breached. If one affects you, we will tell you what happened and what to do, without waiting to be asked.

A word about WhatsApp

This service connects to WhatsApp by driving WhatsApp Web, the same interface you would use on a laptop. It is not built on WhatsApp's official Business API and is not endorsed by or affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

Your use of WhatsApp remains governed by their terms, and automated or bulk messaging can lead them to restrict an account. That is why we pace every send and cap the size of a broadcast. The risk is real and it is yours; we would rather say so here than in the small print.
