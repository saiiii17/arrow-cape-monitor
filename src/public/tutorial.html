One message, every group, thirty seconds

You type it once. We send it to each chat one at a time, with their own name on it.

The thirty-second version

1
Link your WhatsApp Open the WhatsApp tab and scan the code with your phone, exactly as you would to use WhatsApp on a laptop. Your number, your chats. You can unlink whenever you like.
2
Build a list Tick the groups and contacts you send to, name the list — Owners, Charterers, the desk — and press Save. Keep as many lists as you need.
3
Write the message Type it once. Press + Their name and each chat gets their own: "Good morning Meridian" to one, "Good morning Dalton" to the next. Attach up to ten images or files.
4
Check, then send You see exactly who it is going to and the real text with the names filled in, before anything leaves. Then press Send.
5
Watch it land A tick per chat: one for sent, two for delivered, two blue for read — the same marks your phone shows.

If you send the wrong thing

Recall

One button deletes the message for everyone, in every chat it went to. Each chat then shows "This message was deleted", as it would if you had deleted it yourself.

WhatsApp only allows this for about two days, and anyone who already read it has read it. If a chat refuses, you are told which one and why — never left guessing.

Sending from WhatsApp itself

You do not have to open the website. Pick one WhatsApp group as your broadcast group, and anything you post in it goes out to your chosen list. The app replies in that group to say how it went.

Only your own new messages count. Other members posting there, and edits to old messages, are ignored.

Why it sends one at a time

Messages go out one chat at a time, a few seconds apart. The same text fired at fifty chats at once is exactly what WhatsApp treats as spam, and the account it would act against is yours.

For the same reason there is a cap on how many chats one broadcast reaches. Deliberate, not a limitation.

What you need

  • A WhatsApp account on a phone you have with you, to scan the code
  • The groups and contacts already in that WhatsApp — we do not add anyone
  • A browser. On a phone you can add it to your home screen and it opens like an app
