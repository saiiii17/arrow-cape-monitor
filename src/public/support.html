Things you can fix yourself

It says WhatsApp is not connected

Open the WhatsApp tab and press Connect, then scan the code. A session can be ended by WhatsApp itself — if you removed the device under Linked Devices on your phone, it needs scanning again.

One chat failed and the rest went

Usually that chat has moved or been archived in a way WhatsApp will not address. Open it once on your phone, then untick and tick it again in your list.

My ticks are not showing as read

They follow WhatsApp exactly: one tick sent, two delivered, two blue read. If someone has read receipts switched off, you will never see blue, and that is their setting, not a fault.

Recall said it could not delete one

WhatsApp allows deleting for everyone for about two days. After that it refuses, and we tell you which chats rather than pretending it worked.

I have lost my authenticator app

Administrators only. Write to us from the address on the account and we will verify who you are before turning the second factor off.

Reporting something urgent

If you believe someone else has reached your account, or a message went to the wrong people, write to us with URGENT in the subject line and we will deal with it before anything else.

If a message went out wrongly, try Recall first — it is faster than we are, and it works for about two days.

Found a security problem?

Tell us at ops@dialinai.com. We will confirm we received it within one working day, and we will not take action against anyone who reports something in good faith.
